TEST E25 -- two screens, two orientations, ONE button each (designed in the Editor by Bob, 2026-11-01)
 LOOK : screen1 is WIDE (landscape) with one button "Button1" in the middle.
 DO   : tap Button1: the picture turns and screen2 appears TALL (portrait) with "Button2". Tap Button2: it turns back. Repeat a few times.
 PASS : every switch turns the picture, each screen is the right way up and fills the glass, and the tap lands on the button in both orientations.
 FAIL : the picture does not turn, a screen is upside down or mirrored, a tap misses the button, or the board stops after a few switches.
