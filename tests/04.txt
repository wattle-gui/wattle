TEST 04 -- lines and an image (look, then tap the picture)
 LOOK : left: a thin white line, a thick yellow line under it, a red line sloping DOWN to the right,
        and a green line sloping UP to the right near the bottom.
        middle: a cyan vertical line.   right: a picture of a dog with a rose, "tap the picture" under it.
 DO   : tap the picture a few times.
 PASS : lines/picture look right (see above), and under the picture the text counts
        "picture tapped 1", "picture tapped 2" ... one step per tap.
 FAIL : lines or picture wrong, or the counter does not change when the picture is tapped.
