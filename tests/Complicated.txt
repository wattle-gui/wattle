Complicated -- four screens, 320 x 240, landscape: a clock dashboard, a moving trace, settings, and a log with a keyboard
 LOOK   : Screen_1: date, hour:minute, seconds, a bar chart of five days, a gauge, a button. Screen_2: the same clock, a "MAIN POWER" trace and a bar under it. Screen_3 SETTINGS: switch, checkbox, a choice, a slider, a stepper. Screen_4 LOG / ABOUT: a memo, "Rename" and a title.
 DO     : Tap the list on the left to visit every screen. Screen_1: tap the button (new temperatures). Screen_3: switch Alarm on, move the slider low, then tap the button on Screen_1 (an alarm pop-up appears). Choose Voltage / Current / Power (the trace on Screen_2 follows). Tap + and - on the stepper (the screen gets brighter or dimmer). Screen_4: tap Rename, type a name, press Enter. Tap Quit, then Yes.
 PASS   : every screen opens and the list marks the right one; the log in the memo lists what you did (while "Log to memo" is ticked); the new name stays after a restart; Quit blanks the screen. NOTE: the clock shows the right time only if the board has Wi-Fi details (wifi.dat) AND enough memory: this design leaves only about 82 KB free, too little to start Wi-Fi after the Runtime has loaded. The script catches this, prints "clock: Wi-Fi did not start" and carries on, with the wrong time.
