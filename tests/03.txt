TEST 03 -- sliders and progress bars (horizontal and vertical)
 LOOK : left: a slider with a bar under it (both about 1/3 full), "H: 30", "V: 70".
        right: a vertical bar (about 70%, filled from the BOTTOM) and a vertical slider (70%).
 DO   : 1) drag the horizontal slider left and right.   2) drag the vertical slider up and down.
        3) tap "To 0", then "To 100".
 PASS : while you drag, the slider's white thumb follows the finger AND the number in the label
        changes with it (live). When you lift the finger, the matching bar jumps to the same level.
        Vertical: up = more (fills from the bottom). "To 0" empties all four, "To 100" fills all four.
 FAIL : the thumb does not follow, the number does not update, the bar does not match,
        direction reversed, or the buttons do nothing.
