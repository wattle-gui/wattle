TEST 09 -- full keyboard (letters, digits, LF, scrollbar)
 LOOK : a white memo at the top saying "(empty)" and a green button "Type".
 DO   : tap "Type". The keyboard takes over the screen (memo on top, keys below).
        1) type HELLO, press Space, type 123.   2) press Bksp once.
        3) press LF and type more lines until there are MORE THAN 5 lines (LF each time).
        4) a scrollbar appears at the right of the memo: tap the top / bottom END of the bar to move one line, tap the MIDDLE to jump to that place in the text.
        5) press Enter.   Then tap "Type" again, type X and press Esc.
 PASS : each key press shows in the memo at once (only the new letter is drawn, no flicker);
        LF starts a new line; the scrollbar appears after 5 lines and scrolls the text;
        Enter closes the keyboard and the main screen's memo holds the text (several lines);
        Esc closes it and leaves the memo as it was.
 FAIL : keys miss or double-type, memo not updated, no scrollbar, text lost, screen not restored.
