TEST 18 -- LOW_POWER (processor sleeps between touch checks)
 LOOK : "ticks: 0" counting up by itself, a bar stepping 10 % per second, a button "Taps: 0".
 DO   : watch the ticks for 20 s (PERIOD in 18.py is the time between ticks), then tap the button about 10 times (normal taps, not very fast).
 PASS : the ticks keep coming at the PERIOD set in 18.py; each tap is counted ("Taps: N")
        - a tap may feel a little slower than usual (up to ~0.15 s), but none is lost.
 FAIL : the clock stops or runs wrong, the screen freezes/garbles after a few seconds,
        or taps are missed/delayed by more than half a second.
 NOTE : to load another test, run_test.py resets the board first (a sleeping board will not
        answer mpremote otherwise).
