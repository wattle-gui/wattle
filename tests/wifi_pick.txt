TEST wifi_pick -- Pick WiFi (a working network chooser: choice dropdown + secret keyboard + network module)
 LOOK : cyan title "Pick WiFi", a dropdown field "(press Scan)", the line "Press Scan to look for networks.",
        and two green buttons "Scan" and "Connect".
 DO   : 1) tap "Scan": after a few seconds the line says how many networks were found (at most 8, strongest first).
        2) tap the dropdown field and pick one network from the list.
        3) tap "Connect": the keyboard opens, typed characters show as *. Type the password, press Enter.
           (Esc instead gives "Cancelled.")
        4) wait up to 10 seconds: "Connected and saved! IP ..." or "Could not connect. Wrong password?"
 PASS : each step behaves as written; the password is never shown. After "Connected and saved!" the sealed file wifi.dat
        exists on the board (look with: mpremote fs ls) and the board can join by itself with Runtime's wifi().
 FAIL : no networks listed, list does not open, keyboard does not open, screen garbled.
 NOTE : the board needs the "network" module (all ESP32 MicroPython builds have it). Do not photograph the list: it shows real network names.
